# Cadence

A course platform where learners buy courses and can ask for their money back. This glossary fixes the language for that process, so the screens, emails, and code all say the same thing.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's written request to get their money back for a Purchase. Its status is always one of *Requested* (we have not reviewed it yet), *Approved*, or *Declined*, and a Purchase has at most one Requested Refund Request at a time.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Pending Chargeback, Cancellation, "pending"

**Refund**:
The money returned to the learner when a Refund Request is Approved. A Requested or Declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal

**Refund Amount**:
What the learner gets back: always the amount they were actually charged for the Purchase, after PPP discounts and coupons, never the course's list price.

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after the Purchase date, counted in whole days. The 14th day counts in full.

**Refund Eligibility**:
A learner can raise a Refund Request only when the Purchase is inside the Refund Window, they have watched less than 2 hours of the course, the Purchase has no Requested Refund Request, and it is not a claimed team seat.

**Approval**:
The decision to grant a Refund Request. It issues the Refund and ends the learner's Enrollment in the course.

**Chargeback**:
A dispute the cardholder raises with their bank, going around us. It is not a Refund Request, and it must never be used as a name for one.

### Learner-facing wording

- Entry point: "Request a refund"
- Statuses: "Refund requested", "Refund approved", "Refund declined"

## Flagged ambiguities

- "Pending" was used for the *Requested* status. Use "Requested" instead.
- "Cancellation" mixed up getting money back with losing access. Losing access is part of **Approval**; it is not a separate concept.
