# Cadence

A course platform where learners buy courses and can ask for their money back. This glossary fixes the language for that process.

## Language

### Purchases

**Purchase**:
A learner buying a course, recording the amount they were charged.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction

**Enrollment**:
A learner's access to a course.

**Team Seat**:
A place on a course bought through a team licence. Once claimed, it belongs to the team, not to the learner.

**Watched Time**:
The total, across a course, of the furthest point the learner reached in each lesson's video.

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's written request to get their money back for a Purchase. Its status is *Requested*, *Approved* (issues the Refund and ends the Enrollment) or *Declined*. A Purchase has at most one Requested Refund Request at a time; after a Decline, the learner may request again while still eligible.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation, pending

**Refund**:
The money returned to the learner when a Refund Request is Approved.
_Avoid_: Reversal

**Refund Amount**:
What the learner was charged for the Purchase.

**Refund Window**:
The Purchase day through the 14th day after it, inclusive.

**Refund Eligibility**:
A learner may raise a Refund Request only inside the Refund Window, with under 2 hours of Watched Time, with no Requested Refund Request open, and when the Purchase is not a claimed Team Seat.

**Chargeback**:
A dispute the cardholder raises with their bank, going around us. Never use it to name a Refund Request.
